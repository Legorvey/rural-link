# RuralLink Phase 04-06 numerical summary

Generated by `experiments/run_adaptive.py` (seed 42, 5000 shared shadowing and Rayleigh realizations per distance, 1,000,000 MC bits).
Grid 1-12 km, step 0.25 km. UE power 23 dBm, BER target 1e-2, outage target 5%.
Rates are nominal and uncoded (B x log2 M), averaged over realizations with outage counted as 0.

## Reading the labels
- `<1 km`: no coverage at the first tested distance. `>=12 km`: right-censored, true radius unknown.
- **Median PL** (THEORETICAL): deterministic, not a reliability figure.
- **Shadowing** (SIMULATED): log-normal shadowing only, outage <= 5%.
- **Shadowing + Rayleigh, instantaneous CSI** (SIMULATED): per realization the link picks the max-rate option whose Eb/N0 meets the AWGN threshold, then BER is the AWGN BER at that instantaneous Eb/N0 (conditional BER, at most 1e-2 by construction). Outage means no feasible option.
- S0 fixed QPSK 25 RB, S1 adaptive modulation, S2 adaptive bandwidth (QPSK), S3 joint, R0 fixed BPSK 1 RB. All scenarios share the same realizations.

## Average Rayleigh BER without CSI versus conditional adaptive BER

The 4-7% figures reported earlier are bit-level Monte Carlo BERs for a modulation chosen from the slow SNR and then sent through independent Rayleigh fading with no instantaneous adaptation. They are average BERs over the whole fading distribution, so they sit far above the AWGN target. With instantaneous CSI the adaptive link transmits only when the instantaneous Eb/N0 meets the threshold, so served bits have conditional BER <= 1e-2. The price is outage and lower effective rate.
